Desk prepares the next step on an account from one customer model. These are the words in that model. You will see them in Desk, in answers, and in the Account API.

## Accounts and people

An **account** is a company. BuiltBlock does not copy your whole CRM. You add the companies you want, usually from HubSpot.

A **person** is someone involved with an account. People can carry a role, how they are connected, and sentiment when BuiltBlock has that context. The Account API does not return email addresses on people.

## What they want

An **outcome** is a result the customer wants to achieve.

A **use case** is what they need to do with your product to get there. A use case can point at an outcome on the same account.

An **evaluation** is one buying effort on an account: what was at stake and how that effort went.

## What happened

BuiltBlock keeps the calls, meetings, emails, notes, and messages it has linked to an account.

An **episode observation** is a detail from one interaction. A **standing observation** is context that stays useful after that interaction.

## Diagnosis

After BuiltBlock has enough context, an account can carry a diagnosis:

- **Relationship**: customer, prospect, former, or unknown
- **Lifecycle**, and when they became a customer
- **Risk**, with a short reason
- **Coverage**, and whether there is enough recorded conversation
- A written **summary**
- **Usage** windows, current and change, when your organization records usage

A HubSpot lifecycle, or a won deal, still decides the relationship on the book. You can set lifecycle yourself only when HubSpot has not already decided it.

## What connecting a source does

Connecting a source records permission to read it. It does not add every company in that system.

HubSpot is where companies enter. Gong, Granola, Gmail, Slack, and Google Calendar attach calls, notes, mail, messages, and meetings to accounts that already exist. Desk reads that model when it decides what needs attention and what to prepare next.
