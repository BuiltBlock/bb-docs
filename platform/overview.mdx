The Built Block dashboard is your central workspace for designing, connecting, and operating automated workflows. From here you create and organize projects, wire up third-party services, and control every account-level setting — all without leaving your browser. Whether you are a solo developer iterating on a new workflow or a team administrator rolling out integrations across an organization, the dashboard gives you a single place to act.

## Dashboard sections

The dashboard is organized into three top-level sections, each accessible from the left-hand navigation sidebar.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Projects" icon="folder-open" href="/platform/projects">
    A **project** is the primary container for your workflows and resources. Create new projects, organize them by purpose or team, monitor their status, and archive or delete them when they are no longer needed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Integrations" icon="plug" href="/platform/integrations">
    **Integrations** connect Built Block to external services such as Slack, GitHub, or any webhook-compatible tool. Manage credentials, configure triggers, and keep your third-party connections healthy from one place.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Settings" icon="gear" href="/platform/settings">
    **Settings** covers everything at the account and workspace level: generate and rotate API keys, invite team members, assign roles, configure notifications, and review your billing plan.
  </Card>
  <Card title="API Reference" icon="code" href="/api-reference">
    Every action available in the dashboard is also available through the **REST API**. Use your project IDs and API keys to automate provisioning and management programmatically.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Navigating the dashboard

After you sign in, the dashboard loads the **Projects** list by default. Use the sidebar to switch between sections at any time. The sidebar also shows your current workspace name and lets you switch between workspaces if your account belongs to more than one organization.

<Tip>
  Bookmark the dashboard URL for your workspace (e.g., `https://app.builtblock.io/your-workspace`) to jump straight to your projects without navigating from the home page.
</Tip>

## Workspaces

Every Built Block account belongs to at least one **workspace**. A workspace is a shared environment for a team — it owns its own projects, integrations, API keys, and billing subscription. Team members you invite share the same workspace and can collaborate on the same projects according to their assigned role.

<Note>
  Personal accounts have a single workspace that matches your account name. If you need to collaborate with others, invite them from **Settings → Team Members** or ask your administrator to do so.
</Note>

## MCP integrations

Built Block is built with **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** support at its core. MCP integrations expose your workflows as structured tools that AI agents and LLM-powered applications can call directly. You manage MCP integrations from the same **Integrations** section of the dashboard alongside traditional webhook and API-based connectors.
