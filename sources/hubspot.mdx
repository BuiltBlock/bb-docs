HubSpot is the company source. Connecting it lets an admin add specific companies. BuiltBlock does not walk the CRM or backfill every company.

Only an organization admin can connect HubSpot or add companies.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open HubSpot">
    Go to **Settings**, then **Sources**, and open **HubSpot**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in with HubSpot. The status becomes connected when BuiltBlock has permission to read the account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add companies">
    Enter company names under **Add companies**. Separate names with commas or new lines. Select **Add accounts**.

    You can add up to 200 names at a time. BuiltBlock queues them and resolves each name in the background.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A name has to match one HubSpot company. If the name is different, or more than one company matches, that name is not added.

## What gets pulled

For each company, BuiltBlock pulls the company, its contacts, deals, notes, emails, calls, and meetings. Processing continues after **Add accounts** returns. The sidebar shows **Setting up** until the account is ready to ask about.

You can also add a company from [MCP](/mcp/run-account) or from **New chat** if you are an admin. Those paths accept a name, a domain, a HubSpot company id, or every company that has a deal.

## Deals

An admin can add every HubSpot company that has a deal, up to 500 companies. That action is available from [`run_account`](/mcp/run-account), not from the names box on this page. Companies beyond 500 are left off, and the result says the list was truncated.

Connecting HubSpot still does not import the CRM on its own. A daily refresh updates companies you already added. It does not look for new companies.
