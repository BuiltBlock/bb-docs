Gmail is one mailbox for the organization. An admin connects it. BuiltBlock uses that mailbox to find mail with your accounts and to send a prepared customer email from Desk.

Gmail does not create companies.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Gmail">
    Go to **Settings**, then **Sources**, and open **Gmail**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    Sign in with the Google account for the mailbox you want BuiltBlock to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Mail attaches when it matches a contact email or the work domain on an account you already added. A public inbox such as gmail.com is never treated as a company domain. Mail that matches no account stays unmatched until an account exists.

Connecting a different mailbox later clears the previous mailbox history and starts over.

If Google access fails, the source shows an error and offers **Reconnect**.

## Send from Desk

When a Desk card is a customer email, you can edit the recipients and the subject, then send it from the connected mailbox. A reply stays on the existing Gmail thread when BuiltBlock already has a message on that account with the same recipient.

The sent copy is saved like any other email on the account.

<Note>
  BuiltBlock does not send the email until you confirm it in Desk.
</Note>
