Google Calendar is one connection per person. Any member can connect their own primary calendar. The other sources stay admin-only, and they stay one connection for the whole organization.

Calendar does not create companies.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Google Calendar">
    Go to **Settings**, then **Sources**, and open **Google Calendar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    Sign in with the Google account whose calendar you want BuiltBlock to read.
  </Step>
</Steps>

BuiltBlock reads meetings on your primary calendar. A meeting attaches to an existing account when an attendee matches that account. Meetings that only include people at your company are skipped. A meeting that matches no account stays unmatched until an account exists.

The same meeting on two teammates' calendars is stored once.

A meeting card in Desk is for preparing or scheduling. It does not create an event on your calendar.

If Google access fails, your calendar connection shows an error and offers **Reconnect**. Disconnecting your calendar does not remove meetings already stored.
