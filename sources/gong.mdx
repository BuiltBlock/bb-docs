Gong adds calls and transcripts to accounts that already exist. It does not create companies.

Only an organization admin can connect Gong.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Gong">
    Go to **Settings**, then **Sources**, and open **Gong**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the access key">
    Enter the **Access key** and **Access key secret** from Gong.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Select **Connect Gong**. The source is connected when the credentials are saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Calls attach when a participant matches a contact email or the work domain of an account you already added. A call that matches no account stays unmatched until an account exists.

<Note>
  Add the company from [HubSpot](/sources/hubspot) first if you want its Gong calls to land on an account.
</Note>
