Slack adds messages from public channels you select. It does not create companies, and it does not read private channels, direct messages, or group direct messages.

Only an organization admin can connect Slack or change the channel list.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Slack">
    Go to **Settings**, then **Sources**, and open **Slack**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Slack">
    Sign in with Slack and allow BuiltBlock to read public channels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose channels">
    Check the public channels to backfill. New messages, edits, deletes, and thread replies are picked up after that.

    A channel marked **not a member** is one the connection cannot read. BuiltBlock does not join channels for you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A message attaches to an account when one contact email or one work domain matches. Two matches, or a public inbox, stay unmatched until a later sync can attach them.

## Desk preview

A Slack card in Desk can pick one shared public channel, one internal channel, or people recorded on the account. Nothing is posted. Sending a Slack message from Desk is not available yet.
