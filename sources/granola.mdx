Granola adds meeting notes and transcripts to accounts that already exist. Connecting it does not create your customer list.

Only an organization admin can connect Granola or start a sync.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Granola">
    Go to **Settings**, then **Sources**, and open **Granola**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the API key">
    Paste the Granola **API key**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose personal emails">
    Leave **Allow personal emails to create accounts** off unless you want a person who uses a personal address, and who is the only customer on a note, to become an account.

    Personal addresses include Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, and similar inboxes. Turning this on does not change accounts you already added.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and sync">
    Connect Granola. BuiltBlock starts a sync. You can also select **Sync now** later, or **Sync again** after the first run.

    The dialog says transcripts and meetings from the last 24 months are synced and linked to accounts. A sync can take a few minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Notes that do not match an account stay unmatched until an account exists. A note with two or more outside companies is not attached.

<Note>
  Add the company from [HubSpot](/sources/hubspot) first if you want Granola notes to land on it. An admin can also send unmatched Granola notes through [`intake`](/mcp/intake).
</Note>
