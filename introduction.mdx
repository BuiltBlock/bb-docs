Customer teams lose time rebuilding what happened on an account before they can do anything about it. BuiltBlock keeps that context together and carries the work forward.

[Desk](/product/desk) is the product. Open it and you see which accounts need you today, what changed, and the next step already prepared. You spend the time with the customer, not catching up.

BuiltBlock is not a CRM. It does not own your pipeline, close dates, or the system where the deal lives. It keeps the account current and moves the work.

## What you get from Desk

Desk follows an account across the work that actually happens: an evaluation, a stalled onboarding, a renewal that has not been approved.

For each account it manages, Desk does three things:

- **Keeps the account current.** Calls, mail, meetings, notes, and CRM context land on one account, with the people involved.
- **Shows what needs attention.** **Focus** is the short list for today: the account, the job, and the next action.
- **Prepares the next step.** You might open a drafted email, an investigation of what changed, or a note that BuiltBlock is waiting and will check back.

You confirm a customer email before it sends. Desk does not post to Slack, and a meeting card does not create a calendar event.

## The customer model underneath

Desk can only prepare that work because each account has one model: who the customer is, what they want, how they plan to use the product, and what happened along the way. When there is enough history, the account also carries relationship, risk, a written summary, and usage.

You do not have to assemble that model yourself. Connect a source, add the companies you care about, and turn those accounts on for Desk. The [concepts](/concepts) page is the short glossary. The [customer model](/product/model) page shows what has been stored.

## Other ways in

Desk is where the team works. Two other doors use the same accounts:

**MCP** lets Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor ask about the book and, for an admin, add a company or a note. The server is `https://mcp.builtblock.com/mcp`. Sign in with your BuiltBlock account.

**The Account API** at `https://api.builtblock.com` lets your own systems read an account or push a note. Create a key on the **API** page.
