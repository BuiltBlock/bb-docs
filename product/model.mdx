**Customer model** is at the bottom of the Desk sidebar. The page title is **Model**. It shows live counts of what BuiltBlock has stored. It does not list individual account records.

Select a count to read what it measures. From that description you can open a chat with a related question already filled in.

If a count cannot be loaded, the page says it is not available. It does not show zero in that case.

## Imported and read

These counts are the records BuiltBlock has brought in:

- **Source records**: the original data used to build the model
- **Calls**, **Meetings**, **Emails**, **Notes**, and **Messages**: customer interactions that have been read

## Customer context

These counts are the model itself:

- **Accounts**: the companies in the model
- **People**: the people on those accounts
- **Outcomes**: the results customers want
- **Use cases**: what they need to do with your product
- **Episode observations**: details from one interaction
- **Standing observations**: context that stays useful after that interaction

## Retrieval

These counts are how that context can be found when you ask a question:

- **Customer interactions**: calls, meetings, emails, notes, and messages indexed for search
- **Searchable passages**: pieces of customer information indexed by meaning
- **Connected context**: accounts, people, goals, and conversations linked together

These three are separate measures. They are not slices of one total.
