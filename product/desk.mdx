Desk is where you work in [app.builtblock.com](https://app.builtblock.com). It is the reason the rest of BuiltBlock exists.

Without Desk, someone still has to reconstruct the account: the last call, who went quiet, whether onboarding stalled, whether the renewal was approved. Desk holds that history on the account and puts the next step in front of you.

You get three things from it:

- **Less catching up.** Open an account and the current state is already there.
- **A short list for today.** **Focus** names the accounts that need you, the job, and the next action.
- **Work that is ready to move.** A customer email, an investigation, or a check-back is prepared on the account. You review it and send or continue.

## What a morning looks like

Open Desk. **Focus** is the first list: a handful of accounts, each with a short label and the next action. It does not show a score.

Open one account. You see the earlier history, the current state, and the prepared work just above the chat. History stays collapsed until you open it.

The prepared card is one specific next step:

- **A customer email.** Change the recipients and the subject, then send it from the connected Gmail mailbox. BuiltBlock does not send until you confirm. See [Gmail](/sources/gmail).
- **An investigation.** Desk has already looked at what changed, such as who has not signed in or what the last call said, and written what it found.
- **A wait.** The account is blocked on something specific, such as a renewal approval. Desk names what it is waiting on and when it will check back.
- **A task, a note, or a meeting.** A meeting card is for scheduling or preparing. It does not create a calendar event.

A Slack card is a preview. You can pick a public channel or people on the account. BuiltBlock does not post it.

This is the same shape of work from an evaluation through onboarding and renewal. Pre-sales and post-sales accounts both belong on Desk.

## Accounts on Desk

The **Accounts** list is the companies Desk is managing. An account appears under **Setting up** while BuiltBlock is still preparing it.

Desk does not turn itself on. An admin checks **In Desk** for each account under **Settings**, then selects **Save changes**. See [Settings](/product/settings). Until at least one account is on, **Focus** stays empty and the chat shows suggested questions instead.

Each account can be assigned to one person. **Team** is the default. After the first assignment, you see your accounts plus accounts left on the team.

Select **Create work** when you already know the job. Describe it, then confirm the responsibility, the current state, how you will know it is done, and the next action. If that job matches work already open, Desk asks before attaching it.

## Ask from the same place

**New chat** is on the Desk sidebar. Use it when you want to ask across accounts, not only open one:

- **Across the book**: what customers said, bought, and still need
- **Ready to move**: which accounts have a prepared message
- **Where we're stuck**: who is blocked, and what you are waiting on

The conversation stays in the sidebar. You can delete one you own. The same questions are available over [MCP](/mcp/ask). Asking is useful. The daily value of Desk is the list and the prepared step, not the chat alone.

**Customer model** sits at the bottom of the sidebar. It shows what BuiltBlock has stored. It is not a second place to work. See [Customer model](/product/model).
