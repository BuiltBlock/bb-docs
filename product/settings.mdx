Open **Settings** from the account menu. Admins see more tabs than members.

## Sources

**Sources** lists HubSpot, Gong, Granola, Gmail, Slack, and Google Calendar.

An admin connects every source except Google Calendar. Any member can connect their own Google Calendar. See [Sources](/sources/overview).

**Connected** means BuiltBlock has permission to read that source. It does not mean companies have been added. If access is revoked, the source shows an error and offers **Reconnect**.

**Confirm disconnect** stops syncing and removes the credentials. Data already in BuiltBlock stays.

## Vendor context

Admins can open **Vendor Context** and add two URLs:

- **Marketing website**: your public story, positioning, and offerings
- **Product documentation**: features, technical details, and product terminology

Select **Save & refresh**. The status reads **Ready** when BuiltBlock has read the site. **Needs attention** means the last update failed. **Refresh** reads the site again. **Remove source** clears the URL.

## Desk

Admins open **Desk**. The heading is **Accounts in Desk.** Check **In Desk** for the accounts the team works on, and set **Assigned to** for each one. **Team** is the default. Select **Save changes**.

An account stays out of Desk until you check it. Unchecking it leaves the work already created. After the first assignment, each person sees their own accounts plus accounts left on **Team**.

If you are at the account limit, the page links to **Choose a plan** on **Billing** instead of adding another account.

## Profile and team

**Profile & team** has two tabs:

- **Profile**: your name and profile details
- **Team**: invite teammates and manage who belongs to the organization

Admins send invitations from **Team**.

## Accounts

Admins can open **Accounts** to find duplicate companies and merge them. Choose the accounts, then choose the primary account to keep in **Merge accounts**. Interactions, meetings, notes, and contacts from the other accounts move onto the primary account. The other accounts are archived.

## Billing

Open **Billing** to see the trial, the number of managed accounts, and checkout.

A new organization gets a 14-day trial. The page shows the end date. During the trial, adding Desk accounts stays open.

When the trial ends, an admin chooses how many managed accounts to pay for and selects **Continue to checkout**. The number starts at the count of accounts already on Desk, and it cannot be lower than that count. Turn accounts off on the **Desk** tab before you choose a lower number.

After you subscribe, **Update account limit** changes that quantity. **Manage billing** opens the billing portal for the card and invoices.

If a payment fails, adding accounts stays open for 3 days. If you cancel, it stays open until the paid period ends.

Members see the status and the message **Ask an admin to upgrade this organization.** Only an admin can check out or change the limit.

Some organizations are already covered and do not use checkout. The Billing page says so.
