**New chat** on Desk is how you ask questions in the app. You can ask about one company or look for a pattern across accounts.

The chat uses the same customer model as [MCP `ask`](/mcp/ask). An answer can include tables, lists, and links back to the source material, rendered as Markdown.

## Start or continue

Select **New chat** for a new question. Your recent conversations stay in the sidebar. Open one to continue it.

Follow-up questions belong in the same conversation. BuiltBlock keeps the earlier answer in mind, so you can say "make a table of those numbers" or "who were the ones in that answer" without repeating the whole question.

You can delete a conversation from the sidebar. That removes the chat.

## While an answer is running

Tool progress appears above the answer and collapses when the answer is ready. You can leave the page. Reopen the conversation to see the finished answer. One conversation handles one question at a time. Starting a new question in that conversation stops the previous one.

## Add something from the chat

An admin can add a company or paste a note, email, or transcript in the same chat. Those are the same actions as [`run_account`](/mcp/run-account) and [`intake`](/mcp/intake). Adding a company returns when the work is queued, not when the account is ready.

If you are not an admin, ask an admin to add the company or the note.
