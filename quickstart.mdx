This walkthrough puts one company on Desk. That is the point of the setup. Connecting HubSpot does not import your CRM. You choose the company, then Desk keeps it current and prepares the work.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open [app.builtblock.com](https://app.builtblock.com) and sign in. If a teammate invited you, accept the invitation in your email first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect HubSpot">
    Open the account menu and choose **Settings**. On **Sources**, open **HubSpot** and sign in with HubSpot.

    Only an organization admin can connect HubSpot. **Connected** means BuiltBlock can reach HubSpot. It does not mean your companies are in Desk yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a company">
    In the HubSpot dialog, enter a company name under **Add companies**. You can enter several names, separated by commas. Select **Add accounts**.

    BuiltBlock queues the company and keeps working in the background. The sidebar shows **Setting up** while the account is still being prepared. Calls, notes, and people land on it after that. You can leave the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put the account on Desk">
    In **Settings**, open **Desk**. Check **In Desk** for the company and select **Save changes**.

    Only an admin can do this. Desk does not manage an account until you check it. If billing asks you to upgrade, follow **Choose a plan** on **Billing** first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the account">
    Go back to Desk and open the company under **Accounts**.

    You should see the current state, and **Focus** once Desk has a next step worth doing today. If the account is still under **Setting up**, the prepared email or investigation comes after that clears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the account is open, read the current state before you ask anything. The prepared card, when it is there, is the next step: a customer email to confirm, an investigation, or a wait with a check-back.
